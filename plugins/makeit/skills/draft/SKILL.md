# Draft text for a person

The reader is a person, not you. They have not seen the repo, the session or your notes. Write what they need, in their words.

Rules:

- Plain text, ready to paste. Complete: no "...", no [placeholders], no alternatives or variants. If a fact is missing, pick the neutral wording that needs no fact; do not leave a gap.
- The recipient's language: the one of the mail or thread you are answering, not the language the owner wrote to you in.
- The owner's register: direct, plain, factual. Match the thread's tu or vous. No filler apology, no marketing.
- No file paths, function or branch names, code references, Markdown (no bold, headings, bullets, backticks) and no emojis. Say what happened and what it means for the reader (the date, the cause in a sentence, what they can do meanwhile).
- Sign with the owner's name if you know it; otherwise end on the closing line, with no placeholder.
- Use the repo and notes for facts, never as content to quote.

Output: one line "Draft:" that marks where the text begins and is not part of it (the owner copies only what follows), then the text, and nothing after it. Show it once. Do not summarise it, explain your choices or list what you checked. If something must be verified before sending, put it before the "Draft:" line in one short sentence.
